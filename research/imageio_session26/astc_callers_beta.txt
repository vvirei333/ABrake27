## Task 2: Xref on CreateReader_DDS_ASTC

### Result:
  CreateReader_DDS_ASTC (0x187a4f144) — 1 xref:
    0x18781ce44: IIO_ReaderHandler::buildPluginList() + 2104

### Confirmation — CreateReader_ASTC also in buildPluginList:
  CreateReader_ASTC (0x18781e628) — 1 xref:
    0x18781c914: IIO_ReaderHandler::buildPluginList() + 776

### Interpretation:
  Both ASTC reader factories are registered into the IIO_ReaderHandler plugin list.
  This is the AUTODETECT pathway — when CGImageSourceCreateImageAtIndex is called,
  ImageIO iterates buildPluginList entries, calls testHeader() on each, and the
  matching reader's createReadPlugin() instantiates the decoder (ASTCReadPlugin).
  
  The hardened ASTCReadPlugin::initialize code is therefore reachable from ANY
  caller that decodes an image via CGImageSourceCreateImageAtIndex on a file
  whose header matches the ASTC/DDS-ASTC magic bytes.
